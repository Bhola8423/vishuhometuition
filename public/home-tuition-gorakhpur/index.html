Home Tuition in Gorakhpur — FAQs

Which areas of Gorakhpur do you cover?

We receive requirements from major Gorakhpur localities including Taramandal, Rapti Nagar, Mohaddipur, Golghar, Betiahata, Civil Lines, Rustampur, Basharatpur, Shahpur, Padri Bazar, Kunraghat and many nearby micro-localities. Exact matching depends on tutor availability.

Do you provide female home tutors in Gorakhpur?

You can request a female tutor. Matching depends on class, subject, exact locality, preferred timing and suitable tutor availability.

Which classes and boards are supported?

Requirements can be submitted from Nursery, LKG and UKG through Class 12 for CBSE, ICSE and UP Board / State Board students.

Can I get a tutor for Maths and Science?

Yes. You can request Maths, Science, English, Hindi, SST, Computer, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Accounts, Commerce and other academic subjects.

How do I register a student?

Use the “Register Student / Get a Tutor” button. It opens the official student registration flow on the main VISHU HOME TUITION website.

How can a teacher register?

Use “Register as a Teacher” to open the tutor registration form and submit the required details and documents.

Are the displayed areas guaranteed?

No. Area names indicate locations where requirements may be received or checked. Final tutor availability depends on the exact requirement and tutor network at that time.

Do you provide online tuition?

Online tuition can also be requested when appropriate for the class, subject and available tutor.
