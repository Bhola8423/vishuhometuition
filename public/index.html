Helpful Information • Quick Answers

HOME TUITION FAQs

Quick answers about VISHU HOME TUITION in Gorakhpur.

Which classes do you provide home tuition for?

VISHU HOME TUITION supports students from Nursery, LKG and UKG through Class 12th.

Which boards are supported?

Tuition support is available for CBSE, ICSE and State Board students, including UP Board requirements.

How do I request a home tutor in Gorakhpur?

You can use the quick WhatsApp enquiry form or complete the Student Registration form for a detailed tuition requirement.

Do you provide subject-specific tutors?

Yes. Tutor matching can be based on class, board, subject, area and preferred timing.

How can a teacher register with VISHU HOME TUITION?

Teachers can use the “Register as a Teacher” button on this website to open the official tutor registration form.

Where do you provide tuition services?

The service is focused on Gorakhpur city and nearby areas, with tutor availability depending on the student’s location and requirement.

Can I request a female home tutor?

You can mention a preference for a female tutor in your requirement. Matching depends on suitable tutor availability in your area, subject and preferred timing.

What if the first tutor option is not suitable?

Share your concern with VISHU HOME TUITION so the requirement can be reviewed again and other suitable available options can be considered.

Do you provide online tuition as well?

Yes. You can share whether you prefer home tuition or online tuition. Suitable options depend on the class, subject and tutor availability.

How are tuition fees decided?

Fees can vary according to class, subjects, board, tutor experience, location and tutor availability. The displayed fee section should be treated as an indicative reference.

Can I request one tutor for multiple subjects?

Yes. You can mention all required subjects in your enquiry. Matching depends on the student’s class, subject combination and suitable tutor availability.

How soon can I get a tutor?

There is no fixed matching time because availability depends on the requirement, subject, location and preferred timing. After you share the requirement, suitable available options can be coordinated.
